# Privacy Policy for Deep Down Below Zero

**Last updated: 9 November 2026**

Deep Down Below Zero does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal
information.

The game saves your progress — coins, ores, equipment, upgrades and quests — on
your own device. That save file never leaves your device and is not sent
anywhere.

Deep Down Below Zero contains:

- no advertising
- no analytics
- no crash reporting
- no third-party SDKs
- no accounts or sign-in
- no in-app purchases

It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or any other
personal data, and it makes no network requests of its own.

If you delete the app, the save file is deleted with it.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to request, correct or delete.

## Children

The game is rated 4+ and is safe for children. It collects no data from anyone,
including children, and contains no ads, no chat and no links out of the app.
