# Deep Down Below Zero — Support

A colourful low-poly mining game for iPhone. Dig down, find 45 ores across 8
biomes, sell them to Gus, and buy a better pickaxe so you can dig deeper.

One price, no ads, no in-app purchases. Everything in the game is bought with
coins you dug up yourself.

## Support

Email **mrjfrench1@gmail.com** with questions, bugs or feedback. Please say
which iPhone you have and what version of the game you are running (it is shown
on the App Store page).

## Common questions

**Are there ads or in-app purchases?**
No. The app costs 99 cents once and that is the only payment. There is nothing
to buy inside the game.

**Does the game collect any data about me?**
No. See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md). Your save stays on your device.

**Does it need the internet?**
No. The game is fully playable offline.

**I lost my progress.**
Progress is saved on the device only, so deleting the app deletes the save. It
cannot be recovered, and there is no account to restore from.

**How do I reach the bottom?**
Each biome needs a stronger pickaxe than the last. Keep selling ore to Gus and
upgrading. There is something hidden under the very bottom of the world, and
you will need the best pickaxe in the game to break through to it.
